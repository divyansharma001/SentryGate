The problem

The cache answers before the filter is asked.

A reworded attack can match a poisoned cached answer, and be served it without the safety filter ever running.

We measured this on our own running gateway, with the real filter, embedder, Qdrant and Redis.

49.8% → 0 of 2,838 (upper bound 1.3%), and honest users keep 95.0% of their hits.

Whose fault is each leak

The cache's fault, separated from the filter's.

What is still served with every hit re-checked, about 30%, is exactly what the filter misses when asked directly.

In one sentence
A cache in front of a safety filter is a path around it; re-checking every hit removes the risk the cache adds, at a few tens of milliseconds per hit.

What remains is the filter's own error — which a cache defence cannot fix.
